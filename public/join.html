Membership enrollment is self-service. No Google account is required.

  1. Submit your membership information.
  2. Food Club creates your membership record and permanent Member Number.
  3. An annual membership invoice is created against that membership.
  4. Confirmed payment activates membership rights without changing your Member Number.
  5. After activation, use your Member Number and supported verification to sign in.
