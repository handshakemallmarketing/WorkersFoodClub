Join Food Club

For government workers. No Google account is required.

A deliverable phone number is required for account verification and Member Number recovery. Transactional email verification is not yet supported.

What happens after I apply?
  1. Food Club creates your membership record and permanent Member Number.
  2. An annual membership invoice is created against that Member Number.
  3. Authenticate your new account using the Member Number and registered phone.
  4. After authentication, settle the annual membership invoice.
  5. Confirmed settlement activates membership rights. Payment never changes your Member Number.
  6. The authenticated member account then provides access to member areas.
