# SW1-02 observability requirements

When wired to an API, rejected catalog composition should emit typed operational telemetry for closed window, unavailable listing/offer, specification mismatch, place mismatch and non-comparable benchmark. Telemetry must not convert the rejection into canonical evidence by itself and must not leak member identity or employment-verification evidence.

Sales-window configuration audit records must retain actor Participant, authority grant, policy version and governance evidence identifiers.
