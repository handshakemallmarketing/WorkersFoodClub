# SW1-02 member catalog product contract

A member may see a product for an ordering cycle only when the listing is active, the offer is executable at the evaluation time, the offer belongs to the same canonical specification as the listing, the offer pickup place matches the governed sales window, and the window is open.

A benchmark may be shown only when it is a governed executable valuation comparable to the offer's specification, quantity and place. The benchmark is explanatory context, not a promise of savings. Final savings may be zero or negative and is calculated only after fulfillment/remedy economics are known.
