# SW1-02 falsification targets

Reviewers should try to show a member an expired offer through an open window; show an offer after the window closes; combine a listing with an offer for another specification; configure a window with a fabricated/revoked/wrong-scope authority grant; show a benchmark from another specification, quantity or place; or treat the displayed benchmark-member-price difference as realized savings.

Any successful bypass is release-blocking for this slice.
